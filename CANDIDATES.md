## Candidate shapes from an external report

Five shapes proposed in a co-produced research report, held here rather than
entered. Population is still the binding constraint: each needs someone who
can read both literatures.

- `threshold_with_memory` — hysteresis / deadband / bistability / unemployment
  path dependence
- `failure_containment_by_partition` — bulkheads / organelle membranes /
  firebreaks / bulkhead pattern / circuit breakers
- `bottleneck_limited_throughput` — rate-limiting enzyme / processing
  bottleneck / memory wall / well deliverability
- `setpoint_regulation` — homeostasis / thermostat / governor / market
  equilibrium
- `thermal_inertia_buffering` — thermal mass / hibernation fattening / ground
  temperature stability

**Entry gate, not yet passed.** Instances are cited sources, not authored
claims. Some citations in the report are canonical and checkable from the
printed terms (Stoner & Wohlfarth 1948; Gardner, Cantor & Collins 2000;
Kacser & Burns 1973; Wulf & McKee 1995; Maxwell 1868). Others are not usable
as given: a trade website cited as a source, a PMC identifier with no authors,
a PMC number outside the plausible range for its stated year, and two 2026
references that cannot be checked from the terms printed. Until a person reads
them, no instance from this report enters the index.

**The report's structural predictions do not reproduce.** Four of its five
predicted scores are wrong; see `OPEN.md`.
