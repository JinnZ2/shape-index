An entry breaks when its apparent recurrence is explained better by transmission, by a different constraint, or by a different structural signature.

An entry is marked `BROKEN` when its instances turn out to share a citation lineage rather than showing independent recurrence; when the stated constraint does not in fact restrict the claimed geometry; or when the proposed discriminating measurement comes back against the shape.

An entry whose instances are only linguistically similar, with different switch-and-gate structure, is also `BROKEN`. It remains in the file marked as such. Failure records are calibration, not debris to be deleted.
